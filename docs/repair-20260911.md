# 2026-09-11 collection repair invariants

This repair keeps the production contract explicit while the broken first collection is replaced.

- Initial CSV package size is 500 works.
- Candidate identity confirmation and CSV publication readiness are separate decisions.
- A work must not be published as a title-only/sparse record merely because its identity is confirmed.
- Registered works remain researchable through search.wasm identity lookup while missing information is collected; they are not duplicated as new works.
- Known series are expanded to their member works before a series member becomes publishable.
- Series members remain separate work records.
- Existing non-empty values are not destructively overwritten by automatic enrichment.
- Research runs stay bounded and event-chained; CSV creation does not terminate an active research cycle.
- Gemini remains opt-in and disconnected from discovery.
- The incorrect first collected data/state will be reset only after the repaired pipeline passes validation.
